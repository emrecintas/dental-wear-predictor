Prediction of chewing-simulator wear of dental materials by machine learning

This page estimates the volume loss of a dental material in a two-body chewing-simulator test. The estimate is calculated with two models that were trained on 50 published and laboratory tests: a Gradient Boosting model and a hybrid model that combines Archard’s wear law with Gaussian process regression.     4. About the models

    The models were trained on 50 two-body chewing-simulator tests of titanium alloys, Ag-coated titanium alloys, PEEK and resin composites. The accuracy values in the table of Section 2 were obtained by leave-one-out cross-validation. The predictions are reliable only within the range of the training data, and the typical deviation is about ±20 %. Gradient Boosting does not extrapolate beyond the numbers of cycles tested for a material group.
